CTICU Extubation Decision Survey — Group 1

Thank you for participating in this study. This survey is designed to better understand clinical decision-making around extubation in the ICU and how different forms of decision support may influence judgments about patient outcomes.

You will be presented with a series of patient cases and asked to indicate whether, if extubated now, the patient would experience a failure outcome (e.g., reintubation or need for non-invasive ventilation within 48 hours).

The survey typically takes approximately 30 minutes to complete. Please respond based on your clinical judgment.

Thank you for your time and contribution to this research.

If you have any questions or problems please contact tdktrang@vt.edu.
