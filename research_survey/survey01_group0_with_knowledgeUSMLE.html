CTICU Extubation Decision Survey

Thank you for participating in this study. This survey is designed to better understand clinical decision-making around extubation in the ICU.

You will be presented with a series of patient cases and asked to indicate whether, if extubated now, the patient would require reintubation within 48 hours.

The survey typically takes approximately 30 minutes to complete. Please respond based on your clinical judgment.
